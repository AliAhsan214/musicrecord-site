Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information MusicRecord collects, why, and what control you have over it. MusicRecord is a diary for the albums you listen to and the concerts you go to, with an optional social side: following people, a feed, likes, comments, notes and direct messages.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password. If you use Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google instead, the name and email address they share with us (Apple lets you hide your real email), and your Google profile photo if you use Google.
  • Profile: a display name and a handle, created from your name when you sign up, plus the badges you earn and your tier list.
  • What you create: diary entries, ratings and reviews; shows you log (artist, venue, city, date, setlist, notes, and any photos or short videos you add); lists; notes and replies to notes; comments and likes; who you follow; and direct messages, including albums or posts you share in them.
  • Reports you file about other people or their content.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: only if you allow it, and only to find concerts near you. Your approximate coordinates go through our server to Ticketmaster for that one search and are not stored.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, a device token so we can send them to your phone. It is removed when you sign out or turn notifications off.
  • Basic activity: sign-in times, and short-lived counters used to stop spam (how often an account posts, comments, follows or messages). The counters are deleted after a day.
  • A backup of your app data (diary, lists, shows, settings, badges) on your account, so it stays in sync across your devices.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Who can see what

  • Anything you mark private is visible only to you.
  • Public diary entries, shows and lists, and your profile (display name, handle, photo, badges and tier list), are visible to other signed-in MusicRecord users.
  • Notes are visible to the people who follow you, for 24 hours. A reply to a note is visible only to the person who wrote it and the note's author.
  • Direct messages are visible only to you and the person you are talking to. A message from someone you don't follow arrives as a request until you accept it.
  • Photos and videos on a public show are kept in public file storage, so anyone with the direct link can open them, like any photo posted publicly online.
  • To power two badges, we count how many people logged an album and their average rating. These counts never include names or who logged what.
  • If you block someone, the database hides each of you from the other (profiles, posts, notes and messages) and stops follows, likes, comments and messages between you.

Services we use

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, if you use it. Album search and cover art use Apple's public iTunes Search API; only the words you search for are sent, never your account. Push notifications are delivered through Apple's notification service.
  • Expo: relays push notifications to Apple, so it receives your device token and the notification text.
  • Google: only if you choose Continue with Google.
  • Ticketmaster and setlist.fm: for nearby concerts and tour suggestions, through our server. They receive your search (a city, an artist, or approximate coordinates), not your account.

Each of these has its own privacy policy for the data they receive.

Your controls

  • Delete any diary entry, show, list, note, comment or reply, and unsend messages you sent. Posts can't be edited, but you can delete one and post it again.
  • Make new reviews and lists private by default in Settings.
  • Block or report anyone from their profile, their posts, their notes, or a chat.
  • Turn off location or notifications in Settings or your phone's settings at any time.
  • Export your diary, shows and lists as CSV files from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings > Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, follows, notes, conversations (for both people in them), photos, videos and notification tokens. It cannot be undone.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete something, it is deleted; when you delete your account, everything above is deleted with it. Anti-spam counters and queued notifications are deleted within a day.

Children

MusicRecord is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Before you can sign up, we ask for your date of birth. We don't store your birthday; your device only remembers whether the check was passed.

Security

Access rules are enforced by our database itself, not just by the app, so a request for someone else's private data is refused on the server. All traffic is encrypted in transit, and your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top.
